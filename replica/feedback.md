# What users say about the original

**Sample: 51 reviews from 3 sources** (Trustpilot pages 1-2, the App Store listing, an App Store review mirror), read by hand on 2026-11-23. This is below the 100+ target, so every theme here is **thin** and should be re-checked with Google Play reviews before any marketing claim. Trustpilot skews to paid coaching customers; the App Store average (4.6 from 67k ratings) is much kinder than Trustpilot (2.4 from 543). All quotes are verbatim in `reviews.csv`, with links. They are research only, never testimonials.

## What's missing
- New grocery items added quickly (1, App Store mirror).
- Apple Health / device sync (2).
- Reminders that work (1).

## What they like (so we must match it)
- A big Indian food database (4 mentions across Trustpilot and the App Store mirror).
- Macro insight per meal (1).

## Unsolved
- Trustworthy photo numbers: users can't see why the snap chose a value.
- Leaving cleanly: refunds and data deletion need support.
