# Launch plan

1. **Beta first:** Google Play internal testing with 10-20 people Henil knows who track food (mix of Indian and Western diets). Desktop builds shared as GitHub release downloads.
2. **Measure:** add privacy-friendly crash reporting before public launch (e.g. Sentry with PII off). No analytics in v1 on purpose; ask testers directly.
3. **Where unhappy users talk:** r/IndianFitness, r/loseit, r/CICO, r/fitnessindia, Indian fitness Telegram groups. Read rules first; post as the maker, never as a fake user.
4. **Launch post:** lead with the fix: "A free calorie tracker for Indian + Western food, with no upsell, where every number shows its source." Show the photo review screen.
5. **First 10 users by hand:** sit with each for 10 minutes, watch them log a day, write down every hesitation.
6. Keep the original's name out of the name, listing, keywords and ads.
