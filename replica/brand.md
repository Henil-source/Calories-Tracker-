## Logo brief (current mark is a placeholder drawn in code)
- Idea: a plate seen from above with one quarter filled, like a ring chart and a thali at once.
- Type: simple geometric symbol + wordmark.
- Must read at 16 px (favicon) and 1024 px (store icon); one-colour version for notifications.
- Deliverables: SVG symbol, adaptive Android icon (foreground on 108 dp, 72 dp safe zone), macOS/Windows icons, 1024 px store icon, wordmark.
- Must not resemble the original's mark.
