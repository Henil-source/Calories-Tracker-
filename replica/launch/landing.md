**Why people switch** (paraphrased from public reviews of calorie apps, not quotes)
- Paying for a plan and then being pushed to buy more.
- Photo logging that gives a number with no way to see where it came from.

**How it works**
1. Tell Thalika your height, weight and goal. It sets a daily calorie target.
2. Add what you eat: search, pick a portion like "1 roti" or "1 croissant", done.
3. Or snap the plate. Check each item, fix weights, add to your diary.

**Features (fixes first)**
- Free core tracking. No subscription screens, no ads.
- Every food shows its source: Indian Nutrient Databank, ANSES-CIQUAL, USDA.
- Photo estimates you can edit item by item, grounded in the food table.
- Your diary stays on your device. Back it up to a file, delete it in three taps.
- Water, weight and a 7 or 30 day view.
- Android phone app and desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux.

**Pricing:** Free. Photo estimates use your own Claude API key; you pay Anthropic directly, usually a cent or two per photo.

**FAQ**
- *Is it medical advice?* No. It's a food diary. Check targets with a doctor if you're pregnant, under 18 or managing a condition.
- *Does it work offline?* Search, diary and progress do. Photos and packaged-food search need internet.
- *Where's my data?* On your device only, unless you back it up.
- *Why do I need an API key for photos?* So there's no server between you and the model, and no subscription to pay for one.
