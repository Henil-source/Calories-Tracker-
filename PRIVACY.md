# Thalika privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Thalika has no account and no server. Everything you enter (profile, diary, weights, water, your foods, your API key) is stored only on your device.

Data leaves your device only in these cases, and only when you act:
- **Photo estimates.** When you tap "Estimate calories", the photo (shrunk to 1,280 px) and any note you typed are sent to Anthropic's Claude API using your own API key. Anthropic processes it under its own terms and privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Thalika does not see or keep a copy anywhere else.
- **Online food search and barcodes** (can be switched off in Me). Your search words or barcode digits are sent to Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org). No personal data is sent.
- **Backups.** "Back up" saves a file where you choose. Your API key is never included.

Thalika has no ads, analytics or trackers. "Delete all my data" in Me removes everything from the device. Uninstalling the app does too.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
